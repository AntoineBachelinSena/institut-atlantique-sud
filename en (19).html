EUROPEAN TERRITORY IN SOUTH AMERICA

Blue Guyana

The only territory of the European Union in South America, French Guiana has an Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of 350,000 km².

Beyond current hybrid threats

Blue Guyana offers exceptional potential in:

  • • Critical minerals (gold, bauxite, potential rare earths)
  • • Green hydrogen (Hyguane project)
  • • Position as a strategic maritime hub in the South Atlantic

The objective: move from a defensive posture to a proactive sovereignty strategy.

BRAZIL’S MARITIME HEART

Blue Amazon

Brazil’s maritime territory of 5.7 million km², the Blue Amazon concentrates the bulk of Brazil’s economic and energy power in the 21st century.

Structural data

  • 95 % of Brazilian foreign trade
  • 95 % of offshore oil and gas production
  • • Virtually all submarine cables connecting Brazil to the world

Major strategic resource

Critical mineral resources in the depths of the South Atlantic, notably on the Rio Grande Rise — one of the most important polymetallic nodule provinces in the world.
